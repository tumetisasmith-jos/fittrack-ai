FAQ

Common questions

Is FitTrack AI free to use?
Yes — this is a fully functional demo platform. Create an account, log workouts, track your health, and use the AI coach completely free. The platform showcases a complete health-tech product built by Team Vitalis.
How does the AI coach work?
The AI coach analyses your workout history, health metrics, sleep data, nutrition logs, and goals to provide personalised recommendations. Ask it anything — from weight loss plans to recovery advice — and get science-backed, personalised answers.
Is my health data secure?
Absolutely. All data is encrypted with JWT authentication, stored securely, and never shared with third parties. You own your data and can delete it at any time.
Can I cancel at any time?
This is a demo platform with no payment or subscription required. You can create and delete your account freely. All demo data is seeded for demonstration purposes and can be reset at any time.
Does it work on mobile?
FitTrack AI is fully responsive and works beautifully on all devices — desktop, tablet, and mobile. A dedicated mobile app is on our roadmap.
What makes it different from Strava or MyFitnessPal?
FitTrack AI combines workout tracking, nutrition logging, health monitoring, and AI coaching in one unified platform. Unlike Strava (focused on running/cycling) or MyFitnessPal (nutrition only), we give you a complete health intelligence system powered by AI.
